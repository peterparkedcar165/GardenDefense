CACTUS - CURRENT KIT (as of this writing)
===========================================

Base type: Shooter
Family: Ironbark (tank/shield)
Element: Grass
Damage type: Physical

Base stats (CactusData.asset):
  Max Health:     300
  Attack Damage:  12 (per needle)
  Attack Speed:   0.7
  Attack Range:   2
  Armor:          30
  Needle max range: 4
  Skill Cooldown: 35s
  Skill Duration: 15s (base)

-------------------------------------------
PATH 1 - ATTACK: "Needles" (no sub-name set, uses default "Attack")
-------------------------------------------
Fires needles in equal angles around itself (a full 360-degree ring), dealing
Attack Damage (Physical) per needle. Each needle that hits applies 1 stack of
Punctured.

  Needle count = 6 base + 6 per Path1 level (path1NeedlesBase / path1NeedlesPerLevel)
  If an insect is hit by more than one needle in the same volley, every needle
  after the first deals only 50% damage to it (_volleyHit tracking in Shoot/OnNeedleHit).

  Path1 Max Level Bonus: Attacks now apply 2 Punctured stacks instead of 1.

-------------------------------------------
PATH 2 - PASSIVE: "Thorns" (no sub-name set, uses default "Passive")
-------------------------------------------
Whenever an insect deals MELEE damage to the Cactus, it is immediately countered:
the attacker takes damage equal to 100% of its own Attack Damage (Physical,
tagged PassiveDamage + Counter - so it benefits from any generic Counter Damage
stat bonuses), and receives (1 + Path2 level) stacks of Punctured.

  Punctured (the debuff itself, shared across the whole game, not Cactus-exclusive):
  while it has any stacks, taking Physical damage deals an extra damage equal to
  the stack count, as Grass damage. Lasts 8 seconds (Cactus.basePassiveDuration,
  hardcoded in Awake, not read from data). Stacks are additive, capped at 100,
  and refresh/add from any source that applies it.

  Increase Punctured stacks per retaliation by 1 per level.
  Increase Base Max Health by 70 per level (path2HealthPerLevel).

  Path2 Max Level Bonus: Retaliation damage also gains bonus damage equal to
  33% of the Cactus's current Armor.

-------------------------------------------
PATH 3 - SKILL: "Shield" (no sub-name set, uses default "Skill")
-------------------------------------------
Grants the Cactus a shield (CactusShieldEffect) worth 100 + 40 per Path3 level,
lasting 15s + 2s per Path3 level. Can be toggled to Auto Cast: while enabled, the
skill fires automatically the instant it's off cooldown AND at least one insect
is within attack range (HasInsectsInRange check in Update).

  While ANY shield effect holds (not just his own skill's - HasEffect<ShieldEffect>
  is a generic check):
    - Gains 25 + 5 per Path3 level bonus Armor (ShieldArmorBonus).
    - Every 0.25s, every insect within attack range is forced to target the
      Cactus via a brief Taunt effect (ApplyTauntInRange / TauntEffect,
      tauntStrength 2, 0.35s duration, refreshed every tick while shielded).

  Increase shield amount by 40 per level.
  Increase duration by 2 seconds per level.

  Path3 Max Level Bonus: While Shielded (by any source), Total Attack Speed is
  increased by 35%.

-------------------------------------------
NOTES
-------------------------------------------
- Cactus has no skill tree yet (CactusData.asset's skillTree field is empty).
- The "Counter" mechanic (Path2's retaliation) already runs through the game's
  generic DamageTag.Counter pipeline in Entity.Damage(), so any Counter Damage
  stat bonus (fertilizer, future skill tree node, etc.) already multiplies it -
  this isn't wired up specially for Cactus, it's automatic.
- The retaliation only triggers on MELEE hits (OnHitByInsect) - ranged attackers
  are not countered by the current passive.
- Punctured is a shared/global debuff type, not unique to Cactus - other sources
  could in principle apply it too, though currently only Cactus does.
