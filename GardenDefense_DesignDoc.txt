5 (no overcap). tauntValue is a real stat - baseTauntValue (author-set per
PlantData asset, default 3) + tauntValueAdder, floored at 0, feeding the
weighted-lottery targeting system. Every plant also tracks Temperature/comfort
and Air/respiration.

3. SYSTEMS
============================================================

ECONOMY/SUN - fully working. GameManager.AddSun/SpendSun + BonusSunGain
multiplier; new this session: cumulative totalSunGenerated/totalSunSpent
counters feeding the info panel. Generation itself is per-plant (no central
"sun system" class).

LEVELING/EXP - fully working, unchanged. Kill exp = sunDrop/2, split among
attackers (10% floor share).

UPGRADE PATHS - fully working. Cap 5/5/5, no overcap, every active plant
implements all three.

SKILL TREES - infrastructure fully working, content partial: 17 of 26 active
plants have a populated, wired tree (Acorn Knight, Aloe Vera, Begonia, Bog
Iris, Cactus, Calendula, Dandelion, Holly, Leaf Ranger, Nerium Oleander,
Poison Shroom, Rhodiola, Snowdrop, Sunflower, Tansy, Waterlily, Zinnia). 9
have none at all (fileID: 0, not even stale): Bird of Paradise, Anemone,
Kelp, Hellebore, Glowshroom, Gloriosa, Stargazer, Carrot, Old Carrot. Skill
points are per-species (banked from that species' own session exp), not a
global pool.

FERTILIZERS - fully working, two mechanisms: single pre-level pick +
session-only mid-level procedural queue (Common/Rare/Epic = 2/3/4 stats at
1x/2x/3x roll magnitude). New this session: GetCombinedActiveStats() sums
both sources for display.

STATUS EFFECTS/REACTIONS - fully working, deterministic Primer system (the
old random-roll version is dead code). Full matrix confirmed: Fire+Ice =
Fracture, Fire+Grass = Burn, Fire+Poison = Corrosion, Fire+Water = Steam,
Water+Ice = Freeze, Water+Poison = Sludge, Water+Grass = Germinate, Grass+Ice
= Brittle, Grass+Poison = Poisoned, Poison+Ice = Frostbite, Wind+anything =
Windshear.

WEATHER - mostly working, one gap: Sunny/Rain/Snow/Sandstorm/Underwater all
have real mechanical hooks; Windy is flavor-text only, zero mechanical effect
in Plant.ApplyWeatherEffect.

DAY/NIGHT - fully working (DarknessManager), drives night-targeting, Moth's
light-seeking, Oleander's sprout visibility, etc.

LOADOUT - fully working, and not a flat 4 slots: 4 + clamp(highestLevel
Unlocked/5, 0, 4) - 5 slots at level 6 unlocked, up to 8 from level 21 on.

LIVES/HEALTH - fully working, standard.

WAVES/SPAWNING - two coexisting mechanisms confirmed directly: Levels 1-13
are hand-authored (waves[] populated, insects[] empty); Levels 14-27 are
procedural budget/roster-based (insects[]/eliteInsects[] populated, waves[]
empty).

IN-GAME INFO PANEL - new this session, fully working: GameInfoPanel.cs,
self-bootstrapping (zero scene wiring), toggled with G, shows weather,
combined fertilizer stats (alphabetical), and sun totals.
