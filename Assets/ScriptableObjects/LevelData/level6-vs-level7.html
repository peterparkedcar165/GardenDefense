What actually changes

  • Roster swap: Level 6 has Wasp (120 total) and no Mosquito/Snail. Level 7 drops Wasp entirely and adds Mosquito (229) and Snail (73) instead.
  • Length: Level 7 runs 2 waves longer (18 vs 16), and those extra waves are its heaviest — wave 18 alone throws 367 insects, more than any single wave in Level 6.
  • Scout Ant spike: both levels ramp Scout Ant sharply in their last few waves (75 by the final shared wave), but Level 7 keeps climbing afterward, reaching 110 for its last two waves.
  • Overall scale: Level 7 totals 3,469 insects across its run versus Level 6's 2,569 — about 35% more, mostly from running longer and from Mosquito/Snail layering on top rather than replacing anything 1-for-1.
