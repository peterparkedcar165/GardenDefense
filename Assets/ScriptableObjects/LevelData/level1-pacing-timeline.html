Heads up: delayBeforeNext (hand-authored on every sub-wave, in every level this session) is never actually read by ProceduralLevel.cs — only referenced once, in a comment. Sub-waves run strictly back to back (cumulativeStart += sub.subWaveDuration), and a wave's real duration is just the sum of its sub-wave durations. The only actual gap between waves is the level-wide restDuration: 8s constant, applied once after every wave except the last.
