================================================================================
POISON SHROOM — FULL KIT REFERENCE
================================================================================
Family: Wither (debuff / nihility)
Elemental Type: Poison
Damage Type: Magic
Sun Cost: 225

Poison Shroom is a damage-over-time specialist. She deals no direct hit
damage — every point of her Attack Damage is converted into a poison DoT
(Toxic Spore) that ticks on the target over several seconds. Her passive
makes that poison eat away at the target's Poison Resistance while it's
active, her skill blankets an area in a damage field that freezes existing
debuffs in place, and her fully-grown passive turns her into a genuine
crit-based damage dealer on top of the poison.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BASE STATS (Level 1, no path upgrades, no skill tree)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Max Health:            300
Attack Damage:         18
Attack Speed:          0.6
Attack Range:          2.5
Projectile Speed:      2.6      Max Range: 20
Critical Chance:       5%       Critical Damage: 150%
Base Skill Cooldown:   120s
Base Skill Duration:   9s       (Poison Field lifetime)
Base Skill Radius:     2        (Poison Field radius)
Base Poison Field DPS: 8        (+ 11% of Magic Power)
Base Toxic Spore Duration: 3s

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ATTACK  (Path 1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fires a Toxic Spore projectile at the target. It deals NO direct hit
damage — instead it applies the Toxic Spore DoT effect, dealing 100% of
Attack Damage as Poison Magic damage on application and every 0.5s after,
for the current Toxic Spore Duration (base 3s, scaled by Path 2 — see
Passive section below).

Targeting quirk: Poison Shroom avoids re-poisoning insects that already
carry her own Toxic Spore, spreading her attacks across the crowd instead
of stacking onto one target. If every insect in range is already infected,
she instead refreshes whichever one has the least time left on its spore,
right before it would fall off anyway.

Per-level scaling (Path 1, per point):
  +3    Base Attack Damage
  +0.08 Base Attack Speed
  +0.14 Base Attack Range

Level 5 (maxed) bonus:
  Attacks splash onto nearby insects within a 1-unit radius, applying
  Toxic Spore to them as well (implemented in PoisonShroomProjectile.OnHit).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PASSIVE  (Path 2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Base kit (always active):
  While Toxic Spore is active on an insect, it deals bonus damage each
  tick equal to a percentage of the target's CURRENT health — base 1.2%
  per second, +0.4% per Path 2 level. This is read fresh every tick, so
  it naturally tapers off in absolute terms as the target's health drops
  (a shrinking insect takes a shrinking chunk), unlike the flat Attack
  Damage portion of the tick, which stays fixed for the effect's lifetime.

Per-level scaling (Path 2, per point):
  +0.4% Toxic Spore current-health damage per second (on top of the 1.2% base)
  +0.6s Toxic Spore duration (on top of the 3s base)

Level 5 (maxed) bonus:
  Poison Shroom gains the ability to deal Critical Damage with her
  Damage Over Time effects (Toxic Spore ticks and Poison Field ticks can
  now crit) — this is off by default and only turns on once Path 2 is
  fully maxed. She also gains a flat +15% Critical Chance.

Note: this is a full rework from the plant's original kit. Originally,
Path 2 granted a flat Critical Chance + Elemental Affinity bonus (always
on) and the maxed bonus was "+75% DoT Duration". An intermediate revision
had Toxic Spore shred Poison Resistance instead; that too has since been
replaced by the current percent-current-health damage mechanic above.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SKILL  (Path 3) — Poison Field
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Base Cooldown: 120s

Hurls a toxic blob to a targeted area. It lands and creates a Poison
Field with a radius of (Base Skill Radius + Path 3 Radius scaling) that
lasts for (Base Skill Duration + Path 3 Duration scaling) seconds.

Every insect standing inside the field:
  - Takes (Base Poison DPS + 11% of Magic Power) Magic Poison damage
    every 0.5s while inside.
  - Has ALL of its existing negative status effects frozen in time for
    as long as it remains inside the field (their durations stop ticking
    down — they resume counting down again once the insect leaves).

Per-level scaling (Path 3, per point):
  +3   seconds of field duration
  +0.3 field radius

Level 5 (maxed) bonus:
  Each tick of field damage also inflicts a fresh Toxic Spore on the
  insect being hit, so the field seeds her own DoT onto everything
  inside it, on top of the field's own damage.

================================================================================
SKILL TREE — "PoisonShroomSkillTree"
================================================================================
10 steps, 25 skill points total to fully max the tree (level 26 = 25 points).
Node numbering below matches the in-editor node ids (node1..node10).

  1. Toxic Bite                                  [rank 3, 1 pt/rank]
     +2 Attack Damage per level.

  2. Blight Insight                              [rank 1, 2 pts]
     +1 Effective Attack (Path 1) Point — boosts Path 1's own per-level
     scaling as if she'd bought one more real Attack level, without
     spending sun or counting toward "maxed" checks.

  3. Festering Spores                            [rank 3, 1 pt/rank]
     +1 second of Toxic Spore duration per level (flat, stacks on top
     of Path 2's own 0.6s/level duration scaling).

  4. Virulent Insight                            [rank 1, 2 pts]
     +1 Effective Passive (Path 2) Point.

  5. FORK — choose one                           [rank 1, 2 pts each]
     5a. Lingering Toxins
         Insects that step out of the Poison Field early keep taking
         its damage for 2 more seconds after leaving.
     5b. Concentrated Blob
         Poison Field damage +50%, but its radius is reduced by 30%.

  6. Quick Growth                                [rank 5, 1 pt/rank]
     -4 seconds Skill Cooldown per level (up to -20s at rank 5).

  7. Ready to Sprout                             [rank 1, 2 pts]
     Skill is automatically unlocked the instant she's placed (no need
     to pay sun to unlock Path 3 first).

  8. Toxic Insight                               [rank 1, 2 pts]
     +1 Effective Skill (Path 3) Point.

  9. Frugal Spores                               [rank 1, 2 pts]
     -10 Sun cost to place her.

  10. FORK — choose one                          [rank 1, 2 pts each]
      10a. Wilting Grasp
           Insects afflicted by Toxic Spore have their Movement Speed
           reduced by 20% for as long as it lasts. Trade-off: her own
           Attack Speed is reduced by 20%.
      10b. Executioner's Bloom
           Toxic Spore and Poison Field both deal +50% bonus damage to
           insects already below 25% health (an execute). Trade-off:
           her Attack Damage is reduced by 15%.

Cost breakdown: 3 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 5 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 25 points.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (for future reference)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Toxic Spore duration reads Path 2's effective level, a flat skill-tree
  adder (toxicSporeDurationAdder, via StatType.ToxicSporeDurationFlat),
  AND magic-power-independent passiveDuration multiplier:
      ToxicSporeDuration = (baseToxicSporeDuration
                             + path2ToxicSporeDurationPerLevel * effectivePath2Level
                             + toxicSporeDurationAdder)
                            * (1 + passiveDuration)

- Toxic Spore's current-health bonus damage:
      PercentHealthDPS = basePercentHealthDPS
                          + path2PercentHealthDPSPerLevel * effectivePath2Level
  Read once per ToxicSporeEffect instance (captured at application time
  from the source PoisonShroom), then applied every tick in DealTick() as
  target.health * PercentHealthDPS * tickInterval, added on top of the
  flat Attack Damage portion before the Executioner's Bloom execute
  multiplier (if any) is applied to the combined total.

- "Can crit with DoT" is a reference-counted toggle on the entity
  (AddDotCanCrit/RemoveDotCanCrit), only added/removed exactly once when
  IsPath2Maxed flips, tracked via a private _dotCanCritFromPath2 flag so
  it isn't re-added every frame.

- Wilting Grasp's Attack Speed penalty and Executioner's Bloom's Attack
  Damage penalty are both skill-tree unlocks checked live in UpdateStats()
  every frame (SkillTreeManager.HasUnlock), not baked into a stat once.

- Lingering Toxins and Executioner's Bloom's field-side bonus damage are
  both implemented in PoisonField.cs (the skill's AoE damage tick loop);
  Wilting Grasp's slow and Executioner's Bloom's DoT-side bonus damage are
  implemented in ToxicSporeEffect.cs (the attack's DoT effect).
